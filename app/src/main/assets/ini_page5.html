Basic Information

To see your location on the map you will not need internet, but you need to download two types of files in advance.The files are of two types:

  • Maps that contains geographic data about a country or region.

  • Dems (Digital Elevation Maps) that contain altimetric data of the terrain;

Initially the app contains neither Maps nor DEMs. You will be invited to download the map of the place where you are. Later on you can also download more maps.

DEMs are optional and allow an alternative method to obtain altitude and to view level lines. If you keep pressed a point on the screen, you will be guided to download the relevant DEM (covering 1°latitude x 1°longitude). Once you get the DEM you can see the level lines of that area in the same way, by tapping a point on the screen.

For more details see Help / Info.
